# Rapid Response

Rapid Response adds **24/7, 15-minute acknowledgement of S1 issues** to
[Enterprise support](/support/plans). It covers all Momento products and accounts under
your Enterprise agreement.

## Response commitment

For S1 issues submitted through an approved support channel, a Momento staff member
acknowledges your report within 15 minutes, 24 hours a day, seven days a week.
The clock starts when you submit the issue. This is an acknowledgement commitment,
not a time-to-resolution guarantee.

S1 means the product is broadly inoperable. Report incidents through your Enterprise
support channels, Slack or phone, using your existing incident-reporting procedure.
See [support plans](/support/plans#issue-severity) for severity definitions and other
Enterprise response targets.

## Strengthen incident readiness and response

Rapid Response also creates opportunities to improve how your team prepares for and
handles incidents. Depending on your needs, collaboration with Momento can include:

- Reviewing architecture to identify resilience improvements.
- Refining incident runbooks to clarify escalation and recovery steps.
- Coordinating troubleshooting and communication during an incident.
- Reviewing incidents to identify follow-up actions and operational improvements.

These activities are tailored to your needs rather than a fixed set of deliverables
or a scheduled review program.

## Pricing

Rapid Response costs the **greater of $5,000 per month or 2.5% of eligible platform
charges**, in addition to your Enterprise support fee.

The add-on uses the same [eligible-spend calculation as Enterprise support](/support/plans#pricing-details):
infrastructure charges after discounts, excluding professional-services and support fees.
Buying prepaid platform credits does not count as eligible spend; infrastructure charges
paid with those credits do count.

| Monthly eligible platform charges | Monthly Rapid Response fee |
| --- | --- |
| $100,000 | $5,000 |
| $200,000 | $5,000 |
| $400,000 | $10,000 |

## Eligibility and subscription term

Rapid Response requires an active Enterprise support plan and activation of the add-on.
Your existing incident-reporting and troubleshooting-cooperation requirements apply.
Separate workload enrollment, architecture reviews, and alarm integrations are not
required for eligibility.

Coverage and billing begin on an agreed activation date. The add-on is billed monthly
and committed through the remainder of your Enterprise term. It renews alongside
Enterprise unless removed at renewal. Adding Rapid Response does not restart your
Enterprise term.

## Get started

Contact [Momento support](mailto:support@momentohq.com) to request Rapid Response and
agree an activation date. If you need Enterprise support, first follow the
[support-plan activation steps](/platform/how-to/activate-support-plan).

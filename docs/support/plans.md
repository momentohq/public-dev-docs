# Support plans

Momento offers three cumulative support plans: **Basic**, **Production**, and **Enterprise**. A
single plan covers every Momento service in every account associated with your organization.

## Plans at a glance

| Plan detail | Basic | Production | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Included | Greater of $500/month or 10% of eligible infrastructure spend | Greater of $5,000/month or 15% of eligible infrastructure spend |
| Minimum commitment | None | 3 months | 1 year |
| Service SLA | Best effort | 99.9% | 99.99% |
| Support response target | None | S1: 2 hours; S2: 8 hours; S3: 2 business days | S1: 1 hour; S2: 4 hours; S3: 1 business day |
| Support channels | GitHub | Email | Slack and phone |
| Support language | English | English | English |

Higher-level plans include all entitlements from the plans below them.

## Response targets

A support response target measures the elapsed time from when you submit a request through a
channel included in your plan until a Momento staff member acknowledges it. It is not a
time-to-resolution guarantee.

- **S1:** The product is broadly inoperable.
- **S2:** A major feature is broadly inoperable.
- **S3:** A minor feature is broadly inoperable.

A business day is 9:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. Pacific Time, Monday through Friday, excluding United States
federal holidays and Momento company holidays. Contact your Momento account team to confirm the
coverage schedule for S1 and S2 response targets.

When requesting support, provide reasonable details about the issue, reasonable access to the
affected environment when necessary, and reasonable cooperation during diagnosis and resolution.

## Pricing details

Eligible infrastructure spend is the total amount billed for Momento infrastructure after
discounts. Professional-services charges do not count.

Purchasing prepaid platform credits does not itself count as eligible infrastructure spend. When
you later use those credits to pay infrastructure charges, those charges do count. Platform
credits are a payment method, not a discount.

## Platform entitlements

The table lists the entitlement introduced or highlighted at each plan level. Because plans are
cumulative, Production includes Basic entitlements and Enterprise includes both Basic and
Production entitlements.

| Entitlement | Basic | Production addition | Enterprise addition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Compliance label | GDPR | SOC 2 | HIPAA |
| SSO | Google IdP | — | — |
| Team roles | None stated | Owner, Operator, and Viewer | Custom |
| Deployment | Cloud | — | BYOC and self-hosted |
| Metrics | Console | CloudWatch and Prometheus | Request logs |
| Networking | Public gateway | — | VPC Peering, Transit Gateway, and PrivateLink |

The compliance labels reproduce the plan matrix; ask your Momento account team which reports,
agreements, eligibility requirements, and legal commitments apply to your organization.

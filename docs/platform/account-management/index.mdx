# Account management

A Momento **account** has one or more **members**. Each member holds a
[system role](/platform/authentication/roles-and-permissions) — **Owner**, **Operator**, or
**Viewer** — that gates what they can do across the account, auth, and resource categories. Roles are
assigned explicitly to members.

## Members and invitations

An **Owner** can:

- **Invite** a user to the account. An invitation is pending until the user accepts it; the invite
  defaults to the **Viewer** role.
- **Revoke** a pending invitation.
- **Change the role** of another member.
- **Remove** a member.

Membership changes are authorized by the **Owner role itself**. Account-management permissions cover
reading and listing account information; they do not grant access to invite, remove, change roles,
or revoke invitations. No member can change their own role.

## Owner safeguards and leaving

An account always keeps at least one Owner. The last Owner cannot be removed or demoted to another
role.

An Owner cannot use the console's **Leave** action while they hold the Owner role. Another Owner must
first change their role to Operator or Viewer. This also means the sole Owner cannot leave until
another Owner has joined the account. A non-last Owner can be removed as long as another Owner
remains.

## Switching accounts

A user can belong to several accounts and **switch** the active account in the console. After
switching, the session reflects the permissions of the role held in the newly-active account.

## Roles

The roles that gate account membership are the same platform-wide system roles used everywhere else.
See [roles and permissions](/platform/authentication/roles-and-permissions) for what each role can do.
